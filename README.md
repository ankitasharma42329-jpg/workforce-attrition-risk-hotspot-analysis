# Workforce Attrition Patterns & Risk Hotspot Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing workforce attrition patterns and identifying risk hotspots across departments, locations, job levels, and employee characteristics.

The analysis uses HR workforce data to understand where attrition risk is concentrated and which factors are associated with higher employee turnover.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall workforce attrition.
- Identify departments with higher attrition levels.
- Analyze location-based attrition patterns.
- Identify department-location risk hotspots.
- Study the relationship between overtime and attrition.
- Analyze job satisfaction and work-life balance.
- Identify employees with higher attrition risk.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab

## 📊 Key Analysis

The project analyzes:

- Department-wise Attrition
- Location-wise Attrition
- Department-Location Hotspots
- Job Level
- Job Satisfaction
- Work-Life Balance
- Overtime
- Remote Work
- Years at Company
- Monthly Income
- Employee Age

## 🔍 Risk Hotspot Analysis

A risk scoring approach is used to identify workforce segments and employees that show relatively higher attrition-related risk based on selected HR factors.

Visualizations such as bar charts and heatmaps are used to highlight workforce risk patterns.
